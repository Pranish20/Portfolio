The thread
CONNECT

Across these projects, the interface is rarely the whole problem. I look for the human tension underneath it — uncertainty, trust, friction, confidence — then design the system around it.

01 · OBSERVE

Start with what people actually do.

Research, contextual enquiry, usability testing and behavioural signals help separate the stated problem from the experienced one.

02 · FRAME

Find the tension worth designing for.

From neighbour conflict to career confidence, I turn messy observations into a clear design question.

03 · PROTOTYPE

Make the system understandable.

Flows, interaction models and high-fidelity prototypes make invisible rules, states and decisions visible to people.

04 · VALIDATE

Let behaviour change the design.

Testing is not a final checkpoint. It is where assumptions get challenged, scope gets prioritised and the next version becomes sharper.
